Brand and campaign plan

How the relaunch reaches customers: the look, packaging and point of sale, the advert, and how each ad leads to this site. Kids appear drawing, never opening a marker.

Visual branding

Super Tips Refill lockup

Product lockup: the dots of the "i" are ink drops, one per colour that can be refilled. Not the official Crayola logo, which is not used and sits above it on any pack.

Colour

  • Yellow #FFC93C
  • Green #1F9D63
  • Coral (Refill, ink drops) #F2674F
  • Blue #1E63C6
  • Navy #123F86
  • Cream #FFF8E8
  • Ink #25231F

Cream studio base, crayon-stroke accents, thin and thick lines as the recurring motif.

Voice and tagline options

  • Campaign line: "Refill the colour. Keep creating." (from the group's first pitch)
  • Headline system: "Just the blue." / "Just the red." One colour per piece, shown with its own ink drop.
  • Who does what: "Grown-ups refill. Kids draw."
  • Sign-off: "Keep creating."

Short for kids, calm for adults, no promises about money or waste. Group to approve

Creative advert · storyboard

30 seconds: "Keep creating"

Vertical video for short-form platforms. Only children drawing appear; refills and tip swaps are shown by adults' hands, off the child. The edited video is Pending.

  1. 0–4 s

    Draw

    A child draws a big sun; the colour is running faint.

    On screen: "Drawing is the best part."

  2. 4–9 s

    Meet

    The marker floats apart into five parts.

    On screen: "Meet your marker."

  3. 9–15 s

    Refill

    An adult's hands click a colour bottle into the back of the marker. Child out of frame.

    On screen: "Grown-ups handle the refill." + concept note

  4. 15–21 s

    New tip

    A fresh tip clicks in; thin and bold strokes appear.

    On screen: "A new tip. More ways to draw."

  5. 21–27 s

    Create

    Children drawing together, colour everywhere.

    On screen: "Keep creating."

  6. 27–30 s

    End card

    Wordmark and the disclosure line.

    "Student concept — not affiliated with Crayola."

Channels and landing pages

Primary schools and early years: a channel that reaches school buyers and teachers, landing on Schools & nurseries. Meta reaches parents well; reaching schools may need another channel, such as school-supplier listings.
