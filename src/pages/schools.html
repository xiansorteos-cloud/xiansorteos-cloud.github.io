In the classroom

Three steps, once a year

  1. Order with your supplies

    A Classpack of refillable Super Tips with a refill kit for the year, from the school suppliers you already use.

  2. One adult looks after refills

    A named member of staff refills a colour or swaps a worn tip, away from the children, then puts the marker back in the tray.

  3. Pupils just draw

    Refilling is a grown-up job, done away from the children. For pupils, nothing changes except that the markers keep working.

What schools buy today

Crayola already sells a Classpack of 144 Super Tips Washable Markers in 12 colours, described as washable, non-toxic and suitable from age 3 [2]. Super Tips Refill would keep that format and add the refill kit.

Nurseries, preschools and childminders

For the youngest artists (ages 3–4)

Safety comes first

Refilling happens only with adults, away from the children. Before any launch the design would need a safety evaluation: no ink a child can reach, no small parts that come loose, and a closure tested against a recognised standard. We found none written for children's markers, so the nearest route would be ISO 8317, the child-resistant packaging test. What is still untested →

Something to show parents

Fewer markers in the bin is something a setting can share with families, as part of teaching children good habits from the start.

Questions schools ask

Straight answers

How long does a refill take?

The aim is a quick refill that fits between lessons, because staff time is the biggest worry for schools. It has not been timed yet: no prototype exists.

How do we know how much ink to order?

The year refill kit would be an estimate. Refills are planned per colour, in bottles with several doses, so a school could top up only the colours that run out.

Is it still washable?

That is a condition of the design: the same washable ink standard as Super Tips today. It would have to be tested before launch.

Does it cost more?

The starter Classpack is likely to cost more upfront than a standard pack. Refills are meant to make the markers last longer. Prices have not been set.

Can we order it?

No. Super Tips Refill is a student concept, not a Crayola product, and nothing here is for sale.
