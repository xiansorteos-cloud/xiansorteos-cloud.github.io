Concept and evidence

The fun parts of this site are an idea. This page separates what is a fact, what is a choice, what is a guess and what has not been tested — so nobody mistakes one for another.

Four kinds of statement

1 · Facts about today's product

Nothing sourced yet

No verified, cited facts about the current Super Tips have been supplied to this project, so none are stated as fact here. When they are, each will carry its source and scope.

To be documented: size and weight · materials · how the maker describes washability and under what conditions · tip type · packaging · end-of-life guidance for multi-part markers (with source and correct scope).

2 · Decisions proposed by this concept

What the student team chose

  • Split the marker into five parts: cap, replaceable tip, ink reservoir, reusable body, rear plug.
  • Keep the body as the part kids hold; make ink and tip the replaceable parts.
  • Put refills and tip swaps in adult hands, subject to safety evaluation.
  • Show the kit as a non-transactional explorer: no price, no checkout, no data collected.

3 · Hypotheses

Things we think might be true

  • A body that outlives its ink could stay in use longer.
  • Replacing a worn tip could extend a marker's use without replacing the whole thing.
  • Clear separation of parts could make the idea easy to explain to families and schools.
  • Any reduction in plastic can only be assessed by comparing life cycles, spare parts, packaging and transport — it does not follow automatically from being refillable.

4 · Pending tests

Not yet demonstrated

  • Leaks
  • Access to the reservoir
  • Closure and loosening
  • Tip retention
  • Ink exposure
  • Small parts
  • Durability
  • Colour constancy
  • Fine and bold strokes
  • Washability on skin
  • Washability on most washable clothing

None of these has been tested for this design. Ease of opening, safety, leak-tightness, durability, drawing quality and washability are all unproven.

FAQ

Questions grown-ups might ask

Can I refill my Super Tips today?

No. This is a redesign concept, not a feature of the markers on sale, and this site does not explain how to open them. Please don't try.

Can I buy it?

No. There is no product, no price and no availability. The kit page is a pretend explorer only.

Is it safe for children to refill?

No safety claim is made. The proposal is that only adults would handle ink and tips, and only if a safety evaluation supports it.

Will it reduce waste?

Unknown. That needs a comparison of life cycles, spare parts, packaging and transport. No figures are claimed.

Will the ink wash out?

Washability of ink on skin and on most washable clothing has not been tested for this design.

Why does the drawing show threads and ribs?

Only as a visual hint that a part comes off. It is not a claim that a working screw mechanism exists.

Who made this, and is Crayola involved?

It is an academic student concept. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Crayola.
