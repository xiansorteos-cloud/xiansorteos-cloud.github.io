Creative advert · storyboard

30 seconds: "Keep creating"

Vertical video for short-form platforms. Only children drawing appear; refills and tip swaps are shown by adults' hands, off the child. The edited video is Pending.

  1. 0–4 s

    Draw

    A child draws a big sun; the colour is running faint.

    On screen: "Drawing is the best part."

  2. 4–9 s

    Meet

    The marker floats apart into five parts.

    On screen: "Meet your marker."

  3. 9–15 s

    Refill

    An adult's hands top up the ink at a table. Child out of frame.

    On screen: "Grown-ups handle the refill." + concept note

  4. 15–21 s

    New tip

    A fresh tip clicks in; thin and bold strokes appear.

    On screen: "A new tip. More ways to draw."

  5. 21–27 s

    Create

    Children drawing together, colour everywhere.

    On screen: "Keep creating."

  6. 27–30 s

    End card

    Wordmark and the disclosure line.

    "Student concept — not affiliated with Crayola."

Channel idea

Short vertical video (for example Instagram Reels or TikTok) to reach parents; school-facing posts or video where teachers gather for school buyers.
